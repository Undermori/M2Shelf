M2ShelfMobi.exe is a separate replaceable program. M2Shelf communicates through
bounded standard streams; libmobi is not linked into M2Shelf.exe. Encryption
support is disabled. This distribution includes the complete worker source,
build scripts and the GPL/LGPL license texts in M2ShelfMobi-source.zip. You may
modify and rebuild this worker and replace M2ShelfMobi.exe beside M2Shelf.exe.
There is no worker signature restriction preventing that replacement.

The included miniz source retains its permissive copyright/license notice.
See vendor/libmobi/src/miniz.c and miniz.h inside the source archive.
